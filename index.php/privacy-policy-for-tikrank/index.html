Introduction

Welcome to TikRank. This Privacy Policy explains how the app publisher Semina Dağlı collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (“App”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using TikRank, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

Account Information:

  • Anonymous user ID (generated automatically via Firebase Anonymous Authentication)
  • Optional email address (if you choose to provide it for account recovery)
  • Optional username/display name (if you create one in the app)

Video Analysis Data:

  • TikTok video URLs you paste into the app
  • Your saved video analytics and metadata
  • Collections you create and organize
  • Videos you choose to publish to the Discovery feed

User-Generated Content:

  • Videos you publish to our community Discovery feed
  • Any interactions with community content (views, likes)

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Device Information:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • App version and build number
  • Device language and region settings

Usage Analytics:

  • App features you use and how often
  • Screen views and navigation patterns
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • In-app purchase events (subscriptions, transactions)

Technical Information:

  • IP address (temporary, for service delivery)
  • Network connection type
  • Time zone and locale settings

1.3 Information From Third Parties

TikTok Content:

  • We access publicly available TikTok video data when you provide a video URL
  • This includes video metadata (thumbnail, title, author, engagement metrics)
  • All TikTok content remains hosted on TikTok’s servers; we only display analytics

Note: We do NOT require access to your TikTok account. We do NOT log into TikTok on your behalf. We only analyze publicly accessible video data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

2.1 Core App Functionality

  • Analyze TikTok video performance and provide insights
  • Calculate AI-powered viral prediction scores
  • Store and organize your video library
  • Enable video publishing to the Discovery feed
  • Facilitate video boosting (Pro feature)
  • Sync your data across devices via iCloud (if enabled)

2.2 App Improvement

  • Understand how users interact with the app
  • Identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Optimize app performance and loading times
  • Develop new features based on usage patterns
  • Conduct A/B testing for UI/UX improvements

2.3 Communication

  • Send important service announcements
  • Notify you about app updates
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Process DMCA takedown notices

2.4 Subscription Management

  • Process in-app purchases (Pro subscription)
  • Manage your subscription status
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Track conversion and retention metrics

2.5 Legal Compliance

  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect against illegal activities
  • Respond to legal requests from authorities

3. How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the app:

Firebase (Google):

  • Purpose: Backend infrastructure, analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration
  • Data Shared: Anonymous user IDs, usage analytics, device information
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Apple iCloud:

  • Purpose: Optional data sync across your devices
  • Data Shared: Your video library, collections, preferences (encrypted)
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple App Store:

  • Purpose: In-app purchase processing, subscription management
  • Data Shared: Purchase information, subscription status
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3.2 Community Features

When you publish video analytics to the Discovery feed:

  • Your published analytics (thumbnail, metrics, author info) are visible to other users
  • We share this data with our community to enable the Discovery feature
  • You can unpublish your content at any time

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to:

  • Comply with court orders, subpoenas, or legal processes
  • Enforce our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Semina Dağlı, our users, or the public
  • Respond to valid DMCA takedown requests

3.4 Business Transfers

If Semina Dağlı is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change and choices you may have.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where We Store Your Data

Firebase Cloud Firestore:

  • Primary storage for published video analytics
  • Located in US data centers (configurable)
  • Encrypted at rest and in transit

Apple iCloud (Optional):

  • User-controlled sync for personal video library
  • Stored in your personal iCloud account
  • Subject to Apple’s encryption and security policies

On-Device Storage:

  • Video library, collections, and preferences stored locally
  • Protected by iOS encryption and device passcode

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication with anonymous sign-in
  • Access Controls: Limited employee access to user data (need-to-know basis)
  • Regular Audits: Security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure APIs: Backend APIs protected with authentication and rate limiting

Important: While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data we have stored about you
  • Export: Download a copy of your data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Correct: Update or correct inaccurate information

How to exercise these rights: Contact us at info@mobapptech.com

5.2 Opt-Out Options

Analytics Tracking:

  • You cannot completely opt-out of analytics while using the app
  • Basic analytics are necessary for core functionality and bug fixes
  • No personally identifiable information is tracked

iCloud Sync:

  • Disable iCloud sync in app Settings
  • Data will remain stored locally on your device

Community Features:

  • Don’t publish videos to Discovery feed to remain private
  • Unpublish existing content at any time from the Published section

Marketing Communications:

  • We do NOT send marketing emails or push notifications
  • All notifications are service-related only

5.3 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:

  • Right to know what personal information we collect
  • Right to delete your personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do NOT sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

California Resident Requests: Email info@mobapptech.com with subject “CCPA Request”

5.4 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent

Legal Basis for Processing: Legitimate interest (app functionality), consent (optional features), contract (subscription services)

EEA Resident Requests: Email info@mobapptech.com with subject “GDPR Request”

6. Children’s Privacy

TikRank is designed to be family-friendly and is rated 4+ on the App Store. We take children’s privacy very seriously and comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations.

6.1 Information Collection from Children

For Users Under 13:

  • We collect only the minimum information necessary for app functionality
  • We do NOT collect personal identifiable information from children without parental consent
  • We use anonymous Firebase Authentication (no personal data required)
  • Children cannot publish content to the public Discovery feed without parental supervision

What We Collect from All Users (Including Children):

  • Anonymous user ID (automatically generated, not linked to identity)
  • Video URLs pasted into the app (TikTok public content only)
  • App usage analytics (anonymous, for bug fixes and improvements)
  • Device information (model, OS version – for technical support)

What We DO NOT Collect from Children:

  • Name, email, or personal contact information (unless voluntarily provided by parent)
  • Photos or videos uploaded by the child
  • Precise location data
  • Social media credentials or login information

6.2 Parental Controls

Parents and Guardians:

  • You can review and delete your child’s data at any time
  • You can prevent your child from using community features (Discovery feed)
  • You can request a copy of all data associated with your child’s account
  • You can disable iCloud sync to keep data only on the device

To Exercise Parental Rights:

  • Email: info@mobapptech.com
  • Subject: “Parental Privacy Request”
  • Include: Child’s anonymous user ID (found in app Settings)

6.3 Safety Features

We have implemented the following safeguards:

  • No direct messaging or social features
  • Content moderation for published videos
  • No collection of personal information without consent
  • Anonymous authentication (no account creation required)
  • All TikTok content is public and age-appropriate per TikTok’s policies

Age Requirement: Users of all ages (4+) can use TikRank for analyzing TikTok videos. Community features (publishing to Discovery) are monitored for appropriate content.

7. Third-Party Content and Links

7.1 TikTok Content

Our app displays content from TikTok:

  • All video content remains hosted on TikTok’s servers
  • TikTok’s privacy policy applies to their content and services
  • We are not responsible for TikTok’s data practices
  • TikTok Privacy Policy: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

7.2 External Links

The app may contain links to external websites or services:

  • We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites
  • We encourage you to review their privacy policies
  • Opening external links is entirely at your discretion

8. Data Retention

8.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

Active Accounts:

  • We retain your data as long as your account is active
  • Published content remains in Discovery feed until you unpublish it

Inactive Accounts:

  • Accounts inactive for 2+ years may be deleted
  • You will receive email notice (if provided) before deletion

Analytics Data:

  • Aggregated, anonymous analytics retained for 26 months
  • Individual session data retained for 14 months

Legal Requirements:

  • We may retain data longer if required by law or legal proceedings
  • DMCA records retained for 3 years

8.2 Data Deletion

Account Deletion:

  • Email info@mobapptech.com with subject “Delete My Account”
  • We will delete your account within 30 days
  • Some data may be retained for legal/backup purposes (90 days)

Backup Retention:

  • Deleted data may persist in backups for up to 90 days
  • Backups are encrypted and automatically purged

9. International Data Transfers

TikRank is operated from the United States. If you are located outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States.

Data Transfer Mechanisms:

  • We use Firebase, which complies with GDPR and Privacy Shield principles
  • Data transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses
  • By using the app, you consent to this transfer

13. Additional Disclosures

13.1 Do Not Track (DNT)

Our app does not respond to Do Not Track (DNT) signals because we do not track users across third-party websites or apps.

13.2 Cookies and Tracking

We do NOT use cookies. Our app uses local storage and Firebase SDKs for functionality, not advertising trackers.

13.3 Advertising

We do NOT display third-party advertisements. Our only revenue is from Pro subscriptions purchased through Apple’s App Store.

13.4 Biometric Data

We do NOT collect biometric data (face recognition, fingerprints, etc.). We may use device-level biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID) for app security, but this data never leaves your device.

13.5 Location Data

We do NOT collect precise location data. We only use approximate location (country/region) based on your IP address for regional compliance and analytics.
